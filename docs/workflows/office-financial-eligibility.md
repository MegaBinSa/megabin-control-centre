# Office Financial Eligibility Workflow

Authorized Office users can filter current decisions, distinguish stale evidence, preview policy without committing, reevaluate a service, queue bounded reevaluation, inspect route impact, and perform reasoned hold/release actions.

Hold, release and override commands require optimistic versions. Sensitive invoices and payments remain in Accounting and additionally require `accounting.sensitive.read`.

This is an operational decision surface, not a debt-collection CRM. Published routes and active operations are visibility-only.
