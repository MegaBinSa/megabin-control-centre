# Office Live Operations workflow

1. Open **Live Operations** and select an authorised service region.
2. Review the fleet map, authoritative route lifecycle, inferred current context, tracking health, schedule risk, and Needs Attention count.
3. Select a vehicle to inspect authoritative completed stops separately from inferred visits and remaining work.
4. Review each operational fact's type, severity, confidence, explanation, evidence aggregates, vehicle/team, route, and detection time.
5. Acknowledge a fact being investigated, resolve it with an operational reason, or dismiss a false positive with a reason.
6. Refresh or allow 30-second polling to retrieve the narrow live projection. Raw GPS history is never loaded into the Office browser.

Review changes the fact and linked Needs Attention lifecycle only. It cannot edit evidence, Driver outcomes, Route Operations, Published Routes, or tracking observations.
